### <u>📸 Status & Channels</u>

[← Back to main README](../../README.md)

This module replicates the immersive viewing experience of WhatsApp Statuses and the broadcast capabilities of Channels, providing a seamless and highly interactive UI.

### <u>Key Features</u>

- **Immersive Status Player:** A story-style fullscreen status player featuring progress bars, tap-to-skip, and swipe-to-close gestures.
- **Status Ring Indicators:** Real-time visual indicators around profile pictures across the app to denote unseen status updates.
- **Channel Ecosystem:**
  - Dedicated **Channel Explorer** for discovering new content and trending channels.
  - Rich **Channel Post Lists** optimized for high-volume content broadcasting.
  - Detailed channel information panels with follower metrics and quick-action toolbars.

The state is managed efficiently, distinguishing between ephemeral status updates and persistent channel feeds, providing a robust template for broadcast-heavy applications.
