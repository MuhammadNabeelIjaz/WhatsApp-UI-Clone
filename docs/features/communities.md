### <u>👥 Communities</u>

[← Back to main README](../../README.md)

Designed to manage large-scale groups, the Communities module offers an intuitive and scalable structure for community administration.

### <u>Key Features</u>

- **Community Hub:** A centralized dashboard to create, view, and manage broad communities.
- **Sub-group Organization:** Effortlessly nest and categorize multiple sub-groups under a single community umbrella.
- **Announcements Channel:** Dedicated, high-priority announcement channels enabling admins to broadcast messages to all sub-group members simultaneously.
- **Administration & Moderation:** Comprehensive member management tools and community-level privacy settings designed for enterprise-scale administration.

This component emphasizes hierarchical data rendering and scalable state management, perfectly suited for large datasets of nested contacts and groups.
